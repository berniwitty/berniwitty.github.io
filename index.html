基于强化学习与在线蒸馏的数学推理模型后训练GRPO vs. On-Policy Distillation — Post-Training a Math-Reasoning LLM at Matched Compute

技术栈：RLVR、GRPO、OPD、Reverse KL、verl、vLLM、FSDP、Ray、PyTorchStack: RLVR, GRPO, OPD, Reverse KL, verl, vLLM, FSDP, Ray, PyTorch

在同一 Qwen3-1.7B-Base、同一 DAPO-Math-17K 数据和同一评测集上，对比 GRPO 与 OPD 效果，并研究教师模型训练路径对蒸馏效果的影响。On the same Qwen3-1.7B-Base, the same DAPO-Math-17K data and the same evaluation sets, compares GRPO against OPD and studies how the teacher's training route affects distillation.

  • 基于 verl 实现 OPD 与 GRPO，核心差异是监督信号密度：GRPO 依赖 math-verifier 对整条回答产生 0/1 奖励，OPD 则在学生自采样轨迹上利用教师逐 token 的 Reverse KL 提供连续监督。在 GSM8K / MATH-500 / Olympiad-Bench 全量 2493 题上，OPD 准确率均高于等算力 GRPO，相较 Qwen3-1.7B-Base 分别提升 17-26 个百分点。OPD and GRPO both built on verl; the core difference is supervision density: GRPO relies on math-verifier to give the whole answer a 0/1 reward, while OPD supervises the student's own sampled trajectories densely through the teacher's per-token reverse KL. On the full GSM8K / MATH-500 / OlympiadBench sets, 2,493 problems, OPD beats GRPO at matched compute on all three, lifting Qwen3-1.7B-Base by 17 to 26 points.
  • 教师模型选型：控制两个同出自 Qwen3-4B-Base、基础数学能力接近、准确率仅差 2.2 个百分点的教师，差异仅在后训练路径：一个在同域数学数据上直接进行 GRPO，另一个经过完整通用后训练。与学生路径一致的教师蒸出的学生准确率更高，差距随难度递增。Teacher selection: two teachers from the same Qwen3-4B-Base with close math ability, only 2.2 points apart, differ solely in post-training route: one ran GRPO directly on in-domain math data, the other went through full general post-training. The teacher whose route matches the student's distils a more accurate student, and the gap widens with difficulty.

  • 工具调用冷启动：以 Qwen3-Coder-30B 为教师生成 6K 条真实沙箱交互轨迹，经 ReasonFlux-PRM 筛选 3K 条进行 SFT，工具调用作答占比由 15% 提升至 73%。Tool-use cold start: with Qwen3-Coder-30B as teacher, 6K real sandbox-interaction trajectories were generated and filtered to 3K by ReasonFlux-PRM for SFT, raising the share of tool-using answers from 15% to 73%.

  • 训练机制与奖励消融：控制变量对比答案奖励与答案+工具调用奖励两种设置；工具调用奖励使平均调用轮数显著增多，沙箱执行成功率由 66.2% 提升至 79.0%。Training mechanics and reward ablation: a controlled comparison of answer-only reward versus answer + tool-call reward; the tool-call reward markedly increases the average number of calls and lifts sandbox execution success from 66.2% to 79.0%.

  • 基于已规划路径维护轨道节点和区段的时间占用表，合并空闲区间生成安全时间段，在 SIPP 搜索中把状态扩展为“位置 + 方向 + 所在安全时间段”，并用基于曼哈顿距离的启发式引导搜索，避免节点碰撞和对向交换冲突Time-occupancy tables for track nodes and segments are maintained from the planned paths, free intervals are merged into safe windows, and the SIPP search expands states as position + direction + safe interval, guided by a Manhattan-distance heuristic to avoid node collisions and head-on swaps
  • 引入轨道结构约束，对死胡同轨道设置规则，仅在作为起终点或已被占用时允许进入，并采用“带结构约束的优先规划 → 放宽约束的兜底规划”两阶段策略，在高密度路网中兼顾可解率与搜索效率Track-structure constraints are added — a dead-end track may be entered only when it is a start or goal or already occupied — together with a two-stage strategy of constrained priority planning followed by relaxed fallback planning, balancing solvable rate against search efficiency on dense networks
  • 设计 MCP 重规划兜底模块：故障发生时保留所有列车已执行的轨迹前缀，局部微调受影响列车的剩余行程进行排队等待，避免全局重算超时和内存爆炸Designed an MCP replanning fallback: on a malfunction the executed trajectory prefixes of all trains are kept and only the remaining journey of affected trains is adjusted locally into a queue-and-wait, avoiding global recomputation timeouts and memory blowups
