在纯文本电商网站环境里，输入用户需求，输出购买结果，过程中模型自主搜索、点开详情、比较候选、选规格下单。以 ShopSimulator benchmark 的严格成功率为准，商品类目、属性、规格、价格四项同时正确才计一次成功。包含单多轮、个性化用户画像场景。通过多智能体采样数据，后训练单模型，把 Qwen3-8B 自主买对商品的能力从 8.3% 提升到 45.5%。In a text-only e-commerce environment the model takes a user request and returns a purchase, searching, opening product pages, comparing candidates and choosing a specification on its own. 基于强化学习与在线蒸馏的数学推理模型后训练GRPO vs. On-Policy Distillation — Post-Training a Math-Reasoning LLM at Matched Compute

技术栈：RLVR、GRPO、OPD、Reverse KL、verl、vLLM、FSDP、Ray、PyTorchStack: RLVR, GRPO, OPD, Reverse KL, verl, vLLM, FSDP, Ray, PyTorch

本项目在数学推理任务上用 OPD 提升小模型准确率，与 GRPO 做等算力对照，并对 OPD 教师模型选型探索。This project uses OPD to raise a small model's accuracy on math reasoning, compares it against GRPO at matched compute, and explores how the OPD teacher should be chosen.

  • 基于 verl 实现 OPD 与 GRPO，核心差异是监督信号密度：OPD 是学生自采样序列上逐 token 的反向 KL 值，GRPO 是 math-verify 判定整条回答正误的一个标量。OPD and GRPO both built on verl; the core difference is supervision density: OPD supervises every token of the student's own sampled sequence with a reverse-KL value, while GRPO supplies a single math-verify scalar judging the whole answer.
  • 等活跃算力下，OPD 三个基准准确率均超 GRPO，50 步即超其 250 步终点：相对未训练学生提升 17-26 个百分点。评测覆盖 GSM8K / MATH-500 / Olympiad-Bench 全量 2493 题，配对显著性检验。At matched active compute OPD beats GRPO on all three benchmarks, passing its 250-step endpoint by step 50: +17 to +26 points over the untrained student. Evaluation covers the full GSM8K / MATH-500 / OlympiadBench sets, 2,493 problems, with paired significance tests.
  • 双教师同源对照：两者同出自 Qwen3-4B-Base，自身准确率仅差 2.2 个百分点，区别只在后训练路径：一个单域数学 RL，一个多阶段通用后训练。与学生路径一致的教师蒸出的学生准确率更高，差距随难度递增；训练首步二者对学生序列逐 token 对数概率差的均值相差 1.6 倍，通用后训练教师离学生分布更远。Two same-origin teachers compared: both come from Qwen3-4B-Base and differ by only 2.2 points in their own accuracy, differing solely in post-training route — single-domain math RL versus multi-stage general post-training. The teacher matching the student's route distils a more accurate student and the gap widens with difficulty; at the first training step their mean per-token log-prob gap on student sequences already differs by 1.6x, so the generally post-trained teacher sits further from the student's distribution.

  • 在 Flatland/Piglet 环境中实现多智能体 planner–executor 架构：高层根据每列车距截止时间的“时间余量”计算紧迫度并依次调度，低层使用 Safe-Interval Path Planning (SIPP) 为每辆列车规划满足截止时间和故障约束的单智能体轨迹Implemented a multi-agent planner–executor architecture in Flatland/Piglet: the high level ranks urgency by each train's slack to its deadline and schedules them in turn, while the low level uses Safe-Interval Path Planning (SIPP) to plan a single-agent trajectory per train under deadline and malfunction constraints
  • 基于已规划路径维护轨道节点和区段的时间占用表，合并空闲区间生成安全时间段，在 SIPP 搜索中把状态扩展为“位置 + 方向 + 所在安全时间段”，并用基于曼哈顿距离的启发式引导搜索，避免节点碰撞和对向交换冲突Time-occupancy tables for track nodes and segments are maintained from the planned paths, free intervals are merged into safe windows, and the SIPP search expands states as position + direction + safe interval, guided by a Manhattan-distance heuristic to avoid node collisions and head-on swaps
  • 引入轨道结构约束，对死胡同轨道设置规则，仅在作为起终点或已被占用时允许进入，并采用“带结构约束的优先规划 → 放宽约束的兜底规划”两阶段策略，在高密度路网中兼顾可解率与搜索效率Track-structure constraints are added — a dead-end track may be entered only when it is a start or goal or already occupied — together with a two-stage strategy of constrained priority planning followed by relaxed fallback planning, balancing solvable rate against search efficiency on dense networks
  • 设计 MCP 重规划兜底模块：故障发生时保留所有列车已执行的轨迹前缀，局部微调受影响列车的剩余行程进行排队等待，避免全局重算超时和内存爆炸Designed an MCP replanning fallback: on a malfunction the executed trajectory prefixes of all trains are kept and only the remaining journey of affected trains is adjusted locally into a queue-and-wait, avoiding global recomputation timeouts and memory blowups
